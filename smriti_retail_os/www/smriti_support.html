help Frequently Asked Questions
How do I open a cashier shift before billing? expand_more
Go to Sales → Day Open/Close or visit /shift. Click "Open Shift", enter your opening cash balance, and confirm. All billing sessions require an active shift.
How do I add a new product/item? expand_more
Go to Masters → Item Master or visit /item-master. Use the "Add Item" button. For bulk upload, use the Item Master import feature on the same page.
How do I process a sales return? expand_more
Go to Sales → Sales Return or visit /sales-returns. Click "Create Return", select the original invoice, and enter the items being returned with quantities.
Why is the barcode scanner not working during billing? expand_more
Make sure the barcode input field is focused (click inside the billing terminal's search box). Check that the barcode is assigned to the item in the Item Master. USB scanners work as keyboard input — no special driver needed.
How do I view GST reports? expand_more
Go to Reports → GST Reports or visit /reports/gst. Available reports include GSTR-1 Summary, HSN Summary, Tax Collected Register, and more.
How do I reset a user's password? expand_more
Go to Administration → User Management or visit /security-workflows. Find the user, click Edit, and use the "Reset Password" button to send a reset email.
