A. Business Meaning: Incentivizes floor executives and primary customer owners based on sales performance thresholds.

B. Formula Definition:
• Gross Commission = Σ (Commission Ledger active entries for month).
• Net Commission = Gross Commission + adjustments.

C. Threshold Enforcement: If Attributed Monthly Revenue < Min Revenue Threshold, Gross Commission defaults to ₹0.00.

D. Worked Example:
• Total Attributed Rev = ₹150,000 (Threshold: ₹100,000).
• Rate: 2.0% -> Commission = ₹3,000.
• Adjustments: -₹500 -> Net Payout = ₹2,500.

E. Ledger Immutability: Commission Ledger rows cannot be updated. Corrections require reversing matching invoice sequences.
