Explainable Metric: Weeks of Cover (WOC)

Business Meaning:

Measures how many weeks your current stock will last based on recent sales velocities. Helps prevent stockouts and dead capital locks.

Central Registered Formula:
WOC = Current Stock / Weekly Sales Velocity
Worked Example:

If "Indigo Straight-Fit Denim" has **140 pcs** in stock, and the weekly sales velocity is **35 pcs/week**:

WOC = 140 / 35 = 4.0 Weeks of Cover
Action Guide:
  • < 2 weeks: Critical Stockout risk. Trigger reorder or inter-store transfer.
  • 4 - 8 weeks: Optimal cover. Keep monitoring.
  • > 12 weeks: Overstocked. Trigger sales campaigns or return to supplier.

*Note: Production thresholds are configured dynamically in SMRITI PSV Settings to match brand-specific buffers.*

Formula Version: PDT-1.2.0 AITDL Governance Registry

Explainable Metric: Customer Health Score

Business Meaning:

Tracks repeat purchase likelihood and engagement weight across a customer cohort to identify retention trends.

Central Registered Formula:
Health Score = Repeat Purchases (40%) + Visit Frequency (40%) + Campaign Engagement (20%)
Arithmetic Worked Example:
• Repeat Purchases Factor: 100% * 0.40 = 40.0%
• Visit Frequency Factor: 100% * 0.40 = 40.0%
• Campaign Engagement: 100% * 0.20 = 20.0%
Final Score Sum: 40% + 40% + 20% = 100%
Data Sources:

Sales Invoices database, customer visits count registry, and WhatsApp campaign click-through records.

Formula Version: CGE-1.4.0 AITDL Governance Registry
