ⓘ Explain Trial Pipeline Health Score
Formula Transparency & Worked Example — Rule DOC-01
A. Business Meaning:

Trial Pipeline Health Score measures the overall quality of the trial provisioning system. Hum ye check karte hain ki trial activation kitni jaldi ho raha hai aur koi failure to nahi aa raha pipeline mein.

B. Formula Registry:
Formula ID: TR-HLTH-01 | Version: 1.0.0
C. Mathematical Formula Expression:
Success Rate = (Active Trials / (Active Trials + Failed Provisioning)) * 100
SLA Penalty = Min(20.0, Max(0.0, (Avg SLA Hours - 4.0) * 5.0))
Health Score = Max(0.0, Success Rate - SLA Penalty)

E. Interpretation Bands:
  • 🟢 Healthy (>= 80%): Provisioning is stable and SLA limits are met.
  • 🟡 Monitor (50% - 79%): Failed activations are present or SLA delays exist.
  • 🔴 Critical (< 50%): High rate of provisioning failures. Immediate inspection required.
