# COMM-06 — PSV Objection Handling Guide
**Sprint**: PSV-COMM-001 Wave 3
**Audience**: Salesperson (internal reference — not shared with prospect)
**Purpose**: Prepared responses to the 5 most common objections
**Usage**: Review before every demo. Do not read aloud — internalize the logic.

---

## Principle: Objection = Unmet Question

An objection is never a rejection. It is always one of three things:

```
1. Missing information  → they don't understand something yet
2. Unaddressed fear     → they have a concern they haven't voiced
3. Legitimate risk      → they have real business constraints
```

The goal is to identify which one — and respond to that, not to the surface objection.

---

## Objection 1 — "Mere Paas Already ERP Hai"

**What they really mean**:
> "I've already spent money on software. Why do I need more?"

**The misunderstanding to correct**:
ERP tracks what the brand dispatched. PSV tracks what the distributor actually sold.
These are two completely different datasets.

**Response**:

> "Bilkul — ERP hona chahiye. ERP aapki primary sales track karta hai —
> kya dispatch hua, invoice kya bana, stock aapke warehouse mein kya hai.
>
> PSV woh karta hai jo ERP nahi karta:
> distributor ne us stock mein se kitna becha?
> Kitna bacha hai? Kitne hafte ka cover hai?
>
> ERP aapka picture complete karta hai aapke godown tak.
> PSV us picture ko distributor ke godown tak extend karta hai."

**Proof point**: "Show the slide: ERP stops at dispatch invoice. PSV starts there."

**Follow-up question**:
> "Aapka ERP aapko batata hai ki Pune distributor ke paas Size 8 ka kitna stock hai abhi?"
> *(They will say no. That's the opening.)*

---

## Objection 2 — "Data Share Kyun Karun?"

**What they really mean** (usually the distributor saying this):
> "I don't want the brand to have leverage over me."
> OR: "This feels like surveillance."

**The fear to address**:
This is a trust objection, not a technical one. Do not show features. Address the fear first.

**Response**:

> "Yeh sawaal seedha hai — isliye main seedha answer deta hoon.
>
> PSV mein brand ko sirf Sell-Through % aur stock levels dikhte hain.
> Aapki pricing, aapke customers, aapke margins — kuch nahi jaata.
>
> Aur honestly — yeh data aapke liye bhi useful hai.
> Aapko pata chalega ki konsa item 90 din se nahi bika
> aur aapka capital lock hua pada hai.
>
> Yeh mutual visibility hai — surveillance nahi.
> Brand ko wahi dikhta hai jo aap dikhana chahte ho."

**If they push further**:
> "Pilot mein hum milke decide karte hain ki exactly kaunsa data brand ko jaata hai.
> Koi mandatory full disclosure nahi hai."

**Do NOT say**: "We will never share your data with anyone." (Over-promising on privacy.)

---

## Objection 3 — "Yeh Sirf Brand Ke Liye Useful Hai"

**What they really mean**:
> "What's in it for me? I'm the one doing extra work."

**The unaddressed value**:
PSV surfaces information the distributor doesn't have either — dead stock, slow movers,
transfer opportunities. Most distributors discover dead stock only during annual physical audits.

**Response**:

> "Main samajhta hoon yeh concern — kyunki normally brand wali tools
> sirf brand ko data deti hain, distributor ko kuch nahi milta.
>
> PSV mein aapko teen cheezein milti hain:
>
> Pehla — aapka dead stock automatically identify hota hai.
> Aapko annual audit ka wait nahi karna.
>
> Doosra — jab brand ke saath baat karte ho reorder ke liye,
> aapke paas numbers hote hain, arguments nahi.
>
> Teesra — aapko pata rehta hai konsa size kab khatam honewala hai —
> aap proactively reorder karte ho, rush order nahi."

**Proof point**: Use COMM-03 Scenario 2 (Meera, Nagpur — ₹4.1L dead stock recovered).

---

## Objection 4 — "Team Use Nahi Karegi"

**What they really mean**:
> "This is a change management problem I don't want to deal with."
> OR: "The effort to train people is higher than the benefit."

**The real concern**: Adoption, not capability.

**Response**:

> "Yeh valid concern hai — har tool ka yahi hota hai.
>
> Isliye humne PSV ko Excel-first design kiya hai.
> Team ko kuch naya sikhne ki zaroorat nahi —
> jo already Excel use karta hai, woh PSV use kar sakta hai.
>
> Pehle hafte hum saath karte hain — onboarding session 30 minute ka hai.
> Template hum dete hain. Format hum handle karte hain.
>
> Aur pilot ke 30 din mein — agar team weekly upload kar rahi hai,
> engagement metric track hota hai. Agar log use nahi kar rahe —
> hum week 2 mein hi pata kar lete hain aur adjust karte hain."

**Follow-up**:
> "Aapke operations mein kaun distributor se directly baat karta hai?
> Woh ek banda hi kaafi hai pilot ke liye."

---

## Objection 5 — "Pilot Ke Baad Kya Hoga?"

**What they really mean**:
> "Am I signing up for something I can't exit?"
> OR: "Will you pressure me into a contract after 30 days?"

**The fear to address**: Lock-in, hidden commitment, pricing ambush.

**Response**:

> "Seedha bata deta hoon — pilot ke baad teen raaste hain.
>
> Pehla — agar data dikhata hai ki PSV ne value diya:
> hum rollout conversation karte hain — aur more distributors mein extend karte hain.
>
> Doosra — agar kuch specific gap tha — WOC tracking useful tha lekin
> dead stock detection relevant nahi tha — hum specifically wahi fix karte hain
> aur re-pilot option dete hain.
>
> Teesra — agar value nahi mili: aap exit karo. Koi contract nahi.
> Pilot ke baad koi automatic billing nahi.
>
> Pricing conversation pilot review ke baad hogi — data ke saath.
> Bina data ke pricing discuss karna dono ke liye waste hai."

**If they ask for pricing upfront anyway**:
> "Pricing structure hai — pilot ke baad share karta hoon jab numbers samne hain.
> Tabhi conversation meaningful hogi."

## Objection Quick-Reference Card

| Objection | Category | Key Response Move |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| "Already ERP hai" | Missing info | Show ERP vs PSV scope gap |
| "Data share kyun?" | Fear (surveillance) | Address trust first, features second |
| "Sirf brand ke liye" | Unaddressed value | Show 3 distributor benefits |
| "Team use nahi karegi" | Adoption risk | Excel-first design + onboarding support |
| "Pilot ke baad kya?" | Lock-in fear | Three clear exit paths |

---

## Objection Severity Guide

```
Objection about data privacy    → HIGH severity. Stop demo. Address directly. Resume only after resolved.
Objection about ERP             → MEDIUM. Quick 2-minute correction. Continue demo.
Objection about team adoption   → MEDIUM. Address, then offer Week 1 onboarding support.
Objection about post-pilot      → LOW. Address, then reframe toward pilot start.
Objection about pricing         → DEFER. "After pilot — data se baat karte hain."
```

---

## What NOT To Do

```
DO NOT:     Argue with the objection
DO NOT:     Dismiss it as uninformed
DO NOT:     Promise things that aren't in the product
DO NOT:     Answer pricing without COMM-04 structure
DO NOT:     Continue demo over an unresolved trust objection
```
